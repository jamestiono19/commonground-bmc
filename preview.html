Key Partners
Who helps us deliver value?

•  Map & place-data providers

•  BINUS student organisations

•  Campus & Festival organisers

Partners bring reach & data.
Key Activities
What must we do well?

•  Build the 7-screen decision flow

•  Filter > score > fairness > Top 3

•  Verify 30–50 venues near campus

•  Run the 10-group pilot

Key Resources
What do we need?

•  Rules-based matching engine

•  Verified venue database

•  Product team

•  Traffic from shared room links

Value Propositions
Why do groups choose us?

•  Fair: hard limits first, nobody below 60

•  Private: nobody sees your limits

•  Fast: one link, no sign-up

•  Advertisers: brand-safe 18–25 audience

“Satu link. Semua jawab. Tiga pilihan pas.”
Kumpulkan preferensi tanpa debat.
Customer Relationships
How do we keep users?

•  Self-service, no sign-up

•  Automated timer, Top 3 & veto

•  In-app feedback & venue flags

•  Campus student communities

Channels
How do we reach users?

•  Room links: WhatsApp, LINE, Discord

•  Campus QR codes & Festival demo

•  Social media

•  Every room brings 3–7 new people

Customer Segments
Who do we serve?

•  Users (free): BINUS students & friend groups of 4–8

•  Payers: advertisers via ad network – games, apps, e-commerce

•  Later: frequent hosts (CommonGround+)

Cost Structure
What does it cost to run?

•  Development & hosting

•  Data / API use

•  Venue checks & updates

•  Campus marketing

Lean build. Verified data.
Revenue Streams
How do we make money?

•  Phase 1: free pilot, no ads

•  Phase 2: in-app ads (CPM / CPC) on 2 screens only

•  Phase 3: CommonGround+ for hosts, ~Rp15–25k / month

•  Never food ads – picks stay neutral

Free for users. Ads first, subscription later.
